Creamy Roasted Pumpkin Pasta

A silky pumpkin sauce, roasted garlic, and crispy sage come together in a quick dinner that feels cozy without being heavy.

Ingredients

  • 300g pasta of your choice
  • 2 cups roasted pumpkin cubes
  • 4 cloves roasted garlic
  • 3/4 cup cooking cream
  • 1/3 cup grated parmesan
  • 8 sage leaves
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Why People Love It

This pasta is designed for busy evenings: roast the pumpkin in advance, blend the sauce in one step, and finish everything in the same pan.

Flavor Creamy, sweet, and savory
Skill Level Beginner friendly
Best For Family dinner or meal prep
Pair With Lemon salad and sparkling water

Recipe Notice

Spice Level

Mild by default. Add chili flakes if you want extra heat.

Dietary Cautions

This recipe may not be suitable for vegetarians or users with gluten or milk intolerance unless ingredients are substituted carefully.

Step-by-Step Method

Simple visual recipe flow
1
Step 1
Roast the pumpkin and garlic

Toss pumpkin cubes and garlic with olive oil, then roast at 200°C until soft and lightly caramelized.

2
Step 2
Blend a smooth sauce

Blend roasted pumpkin, garlic, cream, a splash of pasta water, and parmesan until smooth. Season with salt and pepper.

3
Step 3
Finish with pasta and crispy sage

Coat cooked pasta with sauce over low heat, loosen with pasta water if needed, and top with fried sage leaves before serving.
