Creamy Roasted Pumpkin Pasta

A silky pumpkin sauce, roasted garlic, and crispy sage come together in a quick dinner that feels cozy without being heavy.

Ingredients

  • 300g pasta of your choice
  • 2 cups roasted pumpkin cubes
  • 4 cloves roasted garlic
  • 3/4 cup cooking cream
  • 1/3 cup grated parmesan
  • 8 sage leaves
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Why People Love It

This pasta is designed for busy evenings: roast the pumpkin in advance, blend the sauce in one step, and finish everything in the same pan.

Flavor
Creamy, sweet, and savory
Skill Level
Beginner friendly
Best For
Family dinner or meal prep
Pair With
Lemon salad and sparkling water

Recipe Notice

Please Check Before Cooking
Spice Level

Mild by default. Add chili flakes or fresh chili only if you want extra heat.

Dietary Cautions

This recipe may not be suitable for vegetarians, people with religious dietary restrictions, or users with common allergies such as gluten or milk intolerance unless ingredients are substituted carefully.

Step-by-Step Method

Photo guide included
Step 1

Roast the pumpkin and garlic

Toss pumpkin cubes and garlic with olive oil, then roast at 200 C until soft and lightly caramelized. This builds the sweetness of the sauce.

Step 2

Blend a smooth sauce

Blend roasted pumpkin, garlic, cream, a splash of pasta water, and parmesan until smooth. Season generously with salt and pepper.

Step 3

Finish with pasta and crispy sage

Coat the cooked pasta with sauce over low heat, loosen with extra pasta water if needed, and top with quickly fried sage leaves before serving.
